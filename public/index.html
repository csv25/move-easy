The person behind Move Easy

A new city. A fresh start. Someone in your corner.

I’m originally from Ecuador, and I’ve called four different countries home. Each move started with a desire to experience more, embrace new opportunities, and live life without wondering, ‘What if?’

Along the way, I’ve learned that moving somewhere new takes more than packing your bags. Choosing a neighborhood, finding the right phone plan, and understanding how everyday life works can make a big difference in how quickly you feel settled. I’ve made mistakes, learned from them, and know how valuable a little guidance can be.

I created Move Easy to help students and young professionals navigate those decisions with greater confidence. I combine firsthand experience with careful research to help you understand your options, avoid common pitfalls, and make choices that fit your priorities.

My goal is simple: to help you feel at home, so you can spend less time figuring everything out and more time enjoying the life you came to build.
